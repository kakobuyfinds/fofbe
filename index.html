How to use this iTaoBuy spreadsheet

Use this page to research a product, then confirm the selected item and purchasing route at the source.

01

Find the matching listing

Choose a product and open CAFANS. Search its exact name and compare its reference, photo and selected option. This page’s shopping buttons lead to the CAFANS homepage.

02

Confirm the platform and fit

Open the product detail page and review its platform options. If you choose iTaoBuy, confirm that the link resolves to the intended listing. Check size measurements, color, quantity and seller terms before paying.

03

Review the complete cost

Confirm the current item price, domestic delivery, service fees and international shipping. Review inspection photos if available, then check the selected shipping route and applicable destination charges.

Before you order

Photos are a starting point.

Listing photos help identify a product, but cannot establish its materials, fit, condition or authenticity. The imported images here are not labeled as warehouse inspections.

For clothing, compare chest width, shoulder width and length against a garment you already wear. For shoes, confirm the sizing system and how any foot or insole measurements were taken.

Your final review checklist

  • Match the original listing to the exact option you intend to buy.
  • Confirm availability and read the current return conditions.
  • Keep source photos separate from any inspection photos provided later.
  • Compare shipping quotes using the same parcel and destination.
  • Save dated prices and unresolved questions alongside the product reference.
Good questions, clear answers

iTaoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is an iTaoBuy spreadsheet?

An iTaoBuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize finds, source links, prices and buying notes. This page presents a small CAFANS catalog snapshot, rather than an official iTaoBuy inventory.

Do the buttons open iTaoBuy directly?

No. Product and shopping buttons on this page open https://cafans.com. Find the matching item on CAFANS, open its detail page and review the available platform options.

Are the displayed prices the delivered cost?

No. They are estimated USD item prices displayed on the CAFANS homepage when imported on 19 November 2026. Shipping, fees and applicable taxes may be additional. Confirm the current listing price and final quote before paying.

Can I download this spreadsheet?

Yes. Use “Download catalog CSV” to export the four imported rows, including CAFANS item references, reference USD prices and the import date. The file opens in spreadsheet applications. It does not refresh itself.

How often does the catalog update?

This page contains a dated static snapshot. It does not automatically synchronize CAFANS stock or prices. Visit CAFANS for its current catalog.

Are these products independently checked?

No. Names, photos and reference prices come from CAFANS listings. Inclusion does not confirm product quality, authenticity or seller reliability. Request the details and evidence relevant to your purchase.
